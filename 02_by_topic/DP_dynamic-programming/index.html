Step-through visual: LCS: fill and trace the DP table

Fill the table one cell at a time. Compare matching characters and see which earlier cells determine each answer.

A match extends the diagonal answer by one. A mismatch keeps the larger upper or left answer. After filling the table, follow the chosen dependencies backward to recover a longest common subsequence.

Step-through visual: 0/1 knapsack: take or skip an item

Watch each item compete for capacity, compare the two choices, then trace the chosen items.

Both taking and skipping an item read the previous row. Taking it adds its value and reduces the remaining capacity by its weight. This prevents reusing an item, unlike unbounded knapsack.
