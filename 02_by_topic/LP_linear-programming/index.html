Step-through visual: LP geometry: constraints and objective lines

Add constraints to reveal the feasible region, then move the objective line to the best feasible value.

For x,y nonnegative, x≤4, y≤3, and x+y≤5, maximizing 2x+y gives value 9 at (4,1). Maximizing x+y instead gives value 5 along the edge from (4,1) to (2,3), so the optimum need not be unique.
